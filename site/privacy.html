Privacy Policy

NORMA · last updated 20 September 2026

NORMA is a personal home-display project run by one person on their own Raspberry Pi. It has a single user: its owner. Nothing it reads is sold, shared, or sent anywhere except to the services the owner connects it to.

What NORMA accesses

When the owner connects a Google account, NORMA requests the Google Tasks scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks). It uses that access to:

  • read open tasks, so the display can show them;
  • mark a task completed, when someone ticks it off on the display;
  • create a task, when someone asks the assistant to remember something.

NORMA requests no other Google scope. It does not read Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Photos, or location history.

Where the data goes

  • Task data is fetched directly by the device on the home network and held in memory to draw the screen. It is not written to a database.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored in a file on the device, readable only by the account that runs NORMA.
  • Nothing is transmitted to the author, to any analytics service, or to any third party. There is no server other than the device itself.

Other services

Depending on what its owner configures, NORMA may also talk to Spotify (playback), Open-Meteo (weather), Yahoo Finance (share prices), Apple iCloud (calendar), and Anthropic or Google (the assistant's answers). Each is used only to draw the display or answer a question asked at the device, under that service's own terms.

Retention and deletion

Nothing is retained beyond what is on screen and the sign-in token. Deleting the token file, or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, ends NORMA's access immediately and permanently.
