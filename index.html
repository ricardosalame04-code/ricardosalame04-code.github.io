NORMA

A wall dashboard and picture frame, running on a Raspberry Pi.

NORMA turns a spare monitor into a home display: photos, the day's calendar, reminders, weather, a clock, an investment summary, a music panel that takes over the screen while something is playing, and an assistant you can ask questions.

What it shows

  • Photos from a folder on the device
  • Calendar events and reminders
  • Current weather and a three-day forecast
  • Portfolio movement, as percentages
  • What's playing on Spotify, with controls

Who it's for

This is a personal project, built and run by one person for one household. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it has no users other than its owner. It runs entirely on a device at home.

Google Tasks

NORMA can connect to Google Tasks so the display shows real reminders, completing one on the display marks it done, and spoken reminders become real tasks. See the privacy policy for what that access involves.
